# Market Hunt Agent Constraints

These constraints guide agent evaluation after raw lineage generation. They are
not compiler seeds and must not affect random selection.

- A narrow MVP is possible for one developer.
- Prefer local-first, privacy-friendly behavior with low operational burden.
- Avoid mandatory integrations, accounts, network effects, marketplaces, and sales teams.
- Avoid regulated medical, legal, financial, tax, engineering, diagnosis, or safety claims.
- Prefer low cognitive load, simple onboarding, minimal setup, and immediate value.
- Avoid generic all-in-one positioning and direct competition with platform giants.
- Prefer calm software, narrow wedges, low maintenance, and sustainable support.
- The product remains useful with low-frequency usage and without addictive loops.
